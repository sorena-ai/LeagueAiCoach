Map Rotation and Farming:
• Once Tier 1 turrets start falling, the ADC and Support should generally swap to the Mid Lane to continue farming safely. This transition allows the Mid Laner to move to a side lane to get solo experience.
• ADCs should know when to rotate to assist in taking towers after the laning phase.
• After the laning phase, the Red Buff should generally be secured by the ADC.
Team Fight Positioning Setup:
• The ADC must use their frontliners (tanks and bruisers) as a buffer or shield. If the frontliners are not in front of the ADC, the ADC is positioned too far forward.
• The ADC must constantly practice kiting and spacing to avoid skillshots and divers.
• Survivability equals DPS; positioning must maximize the time the ADC spends firing damage.
