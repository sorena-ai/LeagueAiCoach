Objective Focus: The Jungler should look to secure the Dragon and Rift Herald as early as possible. Knowing when to take an objective is easier if your Bot Lane has priority and is pushing the enemy in, allowing them to rotate quicker to help. You can often solo these objectives, but assistance is always welcome.
Objective Setup and Vision: Before starting Dragon or Herald, the Jungler should typically sweep the area and drop a Control Ward in the pit. Securing Scuttle Crabs also increases map pressure by providing extra vision, which helps the team track the enemy Jungler.
Ganking and Lane Sync:
• Map awareness is essential for making better decisions about when and where to gank.
• Junglers must sync their pathing with wave states. Ideal gank opportunities arise when an allied lane is freezing near their tower or when the wave is bouncing back (slow push into crash).
• A common mistake is Ganking losing lanes; instead, you should path towards pressure lanes (winning lanes) to snowball their lead.
Invading for Advantage: Invading is an important skill to master, allowing you to steal camps, potentially get kills, and put the enemy Jungler behind.
• Good map awareness and vision are necessary to track the enemy Jungler's movements before invading.
• You can invade a weak early-game champion who needs to power farm (like Fiddlesticks) to delay their Ultimate power spike.
