The Jungler's role in the mid-game is to control the flow of the game.
Vision Denial and Control:
• Junglers should switch their trinket for a Sweeping Lens (Oracle Lens) as the game progresses to deny enemy vision.
• You need to buy and place Control Wards throughout the game, prioritizing objective areas.
• Vision is essential for tracking the enemy Jungler's movements and deciding when to make aggressive or defensive moves. If Dragon is up, the enemy Jungler is likely positioned Bot side.
Objective Timers and Rotations:
• The Jungler should ensure they are not idle; they should path with purpose, moving from camp to camp while setting up for the next objective or gank.
• If major objectives like Dragon, Baron, or Rift Herald are spawning, the Jungler should recall at least 1 minute to 1 minute 30 seconds prior to the spawn time to be ready for the fight.
• The Rift Herald spawns until 19:45. Using it to accelerate a gold lead by taking tower plates is a high priority.
Resource Sharing: After the laning phase, if the Jungler is on the opposite side of the map or dead, laners can take jungle farm.
