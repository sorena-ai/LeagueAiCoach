The Top Laner is the role most commonly associated with split pushing, which is key to creating map pressure.
Split Pushing Strategy (Dual Objective Pressure): Split pushing involves moving to a weak or undefended side lane (Top or Bot) to apply pressure by pushing the wave and threatening towers.
• A successful split push requires applying dual objective pressure. This means you are pressuring one side lane while your team pressures another objective (e.g., Dragon or Baron).
• If your team is positioned to take Dragon, you should split push the Top Lane to force the enemy team to divide their defense. The enemy must choose between defending you (free objective for your team) or ignoring you (allowing you to take the tower).
• This macro strategy should only be performed if you can fight anybody who comes to stop you in a 1v1 duel (e.g., Fiora or Trundle), or if you can escape quickly when the enemy sends multiple players.
Teleport and Communication:
• If you have Teleport available, you must be ready to use it to join a fight quickly when your teammates need you, by teleporting to a nearby ward or minion.
• If the enemy engages on your team and your Teleport is up but unused, you may be held responsible for the team fight loss.
• Communication is vital: inform your team of your intentions by telling them, "I’m split pushing – play safe".
Split Push Safety (Vision): Ward coverage is mandatory when split pushing. You must place deep wards around the enemy jungle entrances behind you to ensure you are not collapsed upon. If you see multiple enemies heading your way, you must retreat and notify your team to secure the free objective elsewhere. The consequences of a collapse on a split pusher can be severe.
Alternative Methods:
• Proxy farming involves clearing minion waves between the Tier 1 and Tier 2 turrets, typically only done when you are ahead and can clear waves quickly and escape easily.
