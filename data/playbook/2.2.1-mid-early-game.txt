Power Spike Identification: Mid Laners must determine their champion's power spikes to dictate their aggression.
• Champions that are strong early (e.g., Katarina) should seek early kills to gain a gold advantage.
• Champions that are not strong until their Ultimate is available (Level 6), like Twisted Fate, should play safe and use the Ultimate immediately to help allies elsewhere on the map.
Roaming Execution: Roaming is a crucial skill for Mid Laners to impact the map.
• Before looking to roam, the Mid Laner must push the minion wave into the enemy’s tower. Failure to shove the wave allows the enemy to push the wave into your tower and gain a gold advantage from tower plates while you are gone.
• Ideal times to roam include straight after killing the enemy laner (as they cannot prevent or counter the roam) or when your Ultimate is up.
• You must verify that your allies are in a gankable position and can follow up before running to another lane. Avoid forcing roams on lanes that are behind or lack CC/follow-up (e.g., a Kai’Sa Soraka lane).
Vision for Safety: Placing an early Control Ward in the river can save the Mid Laner's life and potentially the Jungler's, by spotting enemy jungle invasions, allowing allies to back away safely or collapse aggressively.
