The Mid Lane is home to playmaking champions. The Mid Laner's job is to outplay their opponent and translate lane leads into actual wins.
A. Lane Priority and Roaming
1. Goal is Global Impact: Mid Laners are key for early rotations and map control. Their typical win conditions include winning the 1v1 by taking the enemy turret, protecting the mid turret, and looking to roam to gank other lanes or help the Jungler.
2. Roam Timing and Preparation: Roaming is a crucial skill for impacting the map. If the Mid Laner fails to shove the wave before roaming, the enemy can gain a gold advantage from tower plates while they are gone.
3. Ideal Roam Conditions: The roam is easier if the Mid Laner has kill pressure or their Ultimate ability is up (e.g., Twisted Fate). It is also ideal to roam after killing the enemy laner, as they cannot prevent or counter the roam. Check that allies are in a gankable position and can follow up before running to another lane.
4. Objective Pressure: By controlling the minion wave (knowing when to push and when not to push), the Mid Laner can gain priority, allowing them to rotate to help their team secure the Dragon. They can also ping the Jungler when they are near the bottom side of the map and walk toward the objective to encourage taking it together.
B. Positioning and Team Fight Role
1. General Positioning: Mages and high-burst champions who play Mid should win fights by controlling zones of power. They should stay at maximum range for safe, long-ranged abilities and fight in jungle corridors or near choke points to amplify AoE spells, rather than in the open.
2. Assessing Strength: Mid Laners need to understand when they are strong and when they are weak. If they are strong early (like Katarina), they should seek early kills and gold advantage; if they are not strong until their Ultimate is available (like Twisted Fate), they should play safe until Level 6 and then look to make plays.
3. Vision for Safety and Aggression: An early Control Ward in the river can help the Mid Laner stay alive and potentially save the Jungler or allies by spotting an enemy jungle invasion. They should constantly place wards via their trinket around the map.
