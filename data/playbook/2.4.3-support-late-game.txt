Team Fight Role: The Support role must adapt to the team's needs and their champion's identity.
• Tanky Supports (Engagers/Frontliners) should look to engage the enemy or peel off enemy divers who attack the backline carries.
• Enchanters focus on utility, using items like Locket of the Solari or Redemption to make plays.
• Supports must maximize their champion's power spikes, such as reaching Level 6, which can intensify kill pressure.
Map Awareness and Recall Timing:
• Supports should maintain Control Wards in high-traffic areas and use the Sweeping Lens to deny vision when setting up late-game ambushes.
• The Support should avoid recalling if the team is currently dancing around major objectives like Baron or Elder Dragon. Recalling at these moments gives the enemy a 5v4 number advantage, which can cause the team to lose the fight.
