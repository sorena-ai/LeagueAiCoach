The Jungler operates as the engine of macro play. The role requires being anywhere and everywhere across the map, having the most impact in the game. The Jungler's primary goal is to take control of games from start to finish.
A. Jungle Control and Resource Management
1. Objective Pathing and Purpose: Junglers should path with purpose, ensuring their route is tailored to their champion, their lanes, and the enemy Jungler. Optimal clears are essential for maximizing gold and experience income, allowing the Jungler to gain gold and levels quickly, become more powerful in the early game, and start ganking earlier.
2. Jungle Archetypes: Junglers need to understand their champion's identity to tailor their route. Farmers (e.g., Karthus, Lillia, Master Yi) thrive on full clears to maximize XP and gold, aiming to hit Level 4 or reset. Gankers (e.g., Jarvan IV, Nunu) require early lane impact and should route for 3-camp spikes. Duelists (e.g., Xin Zhao, Udyr) should path to meet the enemy Jungler and fight, prioritizing jungle control over constant ganks.
3. Gold and Buffs: Junglers usually clear camps while positioning for the next primary objective or gank. Most jungle camps grant gold, experience, or individual buffs. The Red Brambleback (Red Buff) provides a burn and slow effect to auto-attacks, while the Blue Sentinel (Blue Buff) offers cooldown reduction and mana regeneration.
4. Invading for Advantage: Invading is an important skill as it allows the Jungler to steal camps, potentially secure kills, and get ahead while putting the enemy Jungler behind. This requires good map awareness and vision to track the enemy Jungler's movements. If the enemy is a weak early-game champion who needs to power farm (like Fiddlesticks), you can invade them early to delay their power spike.
B. Map Impact and Objective Prioritization
1. Objective Control is Paramount: Objective control is one of the most important things for a Jungler to master. You must constantly look to secure objectives such as the Dragon and Rift Herald. Junglers can often solo these objectives.
2. Setting up Objectives: Junglers should prioritize securing vision around objectives like Baron and Dragon. Before starting Dragon or Herald, the Jungler should typically sweep the area and drop a Control Ward in the pit. Securing Scuttle Crabs also provides extra vision, which can help the team know where the enemy Jungler is and prevent the enemy from taking the objective as it is warded.
3. Synchronizing with Lanes: Jungle decisions must sync with lane states. You should clear toward pressure lanes (lanes that are winning) to snowball their lead. If your bottom lane has priority and is pushing, they can rotate quicker than the enemy to help you take the Dragon.
4. Reading the Enemy: Junglers should constantly check the minimap to predict enemy movements and positioning. If the enemy is clearing camps away from an objective (e.g., clearing top side while Dragon is up), you can safely execute the Dragon.
