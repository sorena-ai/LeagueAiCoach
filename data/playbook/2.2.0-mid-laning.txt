The Mid Laner's laning goal is to win the 1v1 by taking the enemy turret.
Laning Mechanics:
• The Level 2 power spike is a crucial spike in Mid Lane, achieved by killing the entire first minion wave and one melee minion on the second wave.
• To successfully trade, fight the enemy immediately after they use a key ability on the minion wave or on you, as their main damage source is temporarily down.
• If you use an ability to clear minions, try to hit the enemy champion simultaneously to gain dual value (push and poke).
Positioning and Zoning:
• Mid Laners should avoid standing in the minion wave against champions with strong wave clear (like Syndra, Orianna, or Sivir). This forces the enemy to choose between pushing the wave or dealing damage to the champion. If the Mid Laner is inside the wave, the enemy can push and poke simultaneously.
• To keep the wave frozen in the middle of the lane (the "yellow zone"), ensure there are equal numbers of allied and enemy minions left alive.
