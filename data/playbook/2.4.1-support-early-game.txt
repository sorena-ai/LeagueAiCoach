Early Roaming Preparation:
• Roaming is highly effective for impacting the map as a Support.
• Supports should purchase early Boots on their first back (even if not upgrading to Tier 2 immediately) to gain extra movement speed, which helps them move around the map quickly to make plays.
• Supports can look to roam straight from base or when their ADC has recalled at the same time and is safe.
• You must ensure that when roaming, you will get something out of it (e.g., kill/assist) and that your ADC will not die while you are gone.
Early Vision Control:
• Supports should prioritize buying a Control Ward on their first back and placing it in a neutral or defensive area (e.g., river). This Control Ward can help spot the enemy Jungler's movements.
• Supports must never stack wards (have 2 available in the trinket slot) in their inventory, as this provides no information to the team.
• Placing wards frequently in high-traffic areas helps spot enemy champions and can save an ally's life.
