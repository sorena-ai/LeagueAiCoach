Translating Lane Priority into Objectives:
• The Mid Laner must use lane control to gain priority, allowing them to rotate to help their team secure objectives like the Dragon.
• If you are constantly pushed in, the enemy has priority, and you will not be able to rotate to help your team.
• After killing the enemy laner, the Mid Laner should hard shove the wave into the enemy tower to deal damage to the turret and collect turret plates for additional gold.
• The Mid Laner can force the Jungler to take an objective by pinging the objective and walking toward it when the Jungler is near the bottom side of the map.
Post-Laning Rotations:
• Once Tier 1 towers fall, the Mid Laner often moves to a side lane to catch minion waves, maximizing solo XP and gold, while the ADC/Support move to the Mid Lane.
• When side-laning, Mid Laners who specialize in quick kills (like assassins) can rotate through the enemy jungle to look for picks (isolated enemy targets).
