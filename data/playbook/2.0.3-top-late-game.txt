The Top Laner’s role in late-game team fights is determined by their champion's function. They are primarily Frontliners who anchor and shape the fight for the rest of the team.
Tanks (Engagers/Frontliners):
• Tanks (e.g., Malphite, Maokai) must be at the front of the team to anchor the fight and soak up damage.
• Their main function is to either engage the enemy team or peel (protect) allied backline carries from enemy divers.
• They excel at setting up powerful AoE wombo combos for the team.
• The ultimate decision is Peel vs. Engage: if allied carries are being dove, the Frontliner peels; if the enemy is immobile and clumped, the Frontliner engages.
Fighters/Bruisers:
• Fighters (e.g., Kled, Darius) should position towards the front of the team.
• They need to prioritize taking down the closest safe target.
• Bruisers must determine if they are strong enough to destroy the enemy backline on their own, or if they need to focus the frontline and protect their own carries.
