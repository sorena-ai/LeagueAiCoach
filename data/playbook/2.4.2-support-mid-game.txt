Mid-Game Vision Denial:
• Supports must facilitate vision control more than other roles.
• Around Level 9, Supports should switch their Yellow Trinket to an Oracle Lens (Sweeper) to deny enemy vision when roaming or setting up objectives.
• Supports must continue to buy and place Control Wards frequently as the game develops.
Objective Rotations and Setup:
• Supports have the second most impact in securing objectives because they can leave the lane to help their Jungler.
• You should be looking to secure Drakes whenever possible.
• A good time to take Dragon is after securing a kill on the enemy Bot Lane and pushing the minion wave.
• When setting up major objectives, the Support should help establish vision 90 seconds before spawn by sweeping the area and placing Control Wards in the pit and surrounding bushes.
