Early Power Spikes: Top Laners must focus on hitting Level 2 first, as this is a crucial early-game spike offering playmaking potential. In the solo lane, Level 2 is achieved by killing the entire first minion wave followed by just one melee minion on the second wave.
Jungle Awareness and Safety: You should always be aware of the general whereabouts of the enemy Jungler at all times.
• Be aware of common enemy jungle routes, specifically the possibility of a Level 3 gank on the Top Lane.
• Knowing if the enemy Jungler is a weak early-game champion (e.g., Malphite or Fiddlesticks) means you can safely assume they will not be ganking early, as they require their Ultimate ability (Level 6) to come online. You can use this knowledge to play aggressively.
Recall Timing for Objectives: If there is a major objective, such as the Dragon or Rift Herald, spawning soon, and you need to recall, ensure you do so at least 1 minute to 1 minute 30 seconds before the objective spawns. This ensures you have fresh items, health, and wards upon arriving to group or contest the objective.
